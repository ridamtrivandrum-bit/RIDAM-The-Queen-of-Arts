Indian Heritage

Classical Dance

Rooted in timeless Indian tradition—master Bharatanatyam, Mohiniyattam, and Kuchipudi foundations, rhythmic Adavus, Hasta Mudras, and expressive Abhinaya.

Beginner Adavus to Stage Margam & Arangettam prep
Dedicated School & College Kalolsavam item coaching
Graceful posture & wellness batches for adult women
High Energy & Stage

Cinematic Dance

Dynamic choreography blending South Indian Cinematic, Bollywood, Contemporary, and Freestyle moves—building stamina, rhythm, and stage charisma.

Solo & Group choreography for School/College fests
Fun cardio-dance fitness for Working Women & Housewives
Body isolation, musicality & camera confidence
Classical Vocal

Carnatic Music

Structured vocal training in Sarali Varisai, Alankaram, Geethams, Varnams, and Keerthanams—cultivating Shruti alignment, Raga depth, and Tala precision.

Voice culture, breath control & pitch accuracy
Light music, Bhajans & Kalolsavam vocal competition prep
Open to children, adults & seniors (No age limit)
Visual & Fine Arts

Drawing Class

Unlock visual imagination through Pencil Sketching, Shading, Watercolours, Acrylic Painting, Oil Pastels, and Traditional Kerala Mural techniques.

Perspective, anatomy, color theory & live sketching
Painting & cartooning competition training for students
Therapeutic weekend art sessions for adults & homemakers
Instrumental

Piano Class

Learn Piano & Electronic Keyboard from scratch—covering Staff Notation, Sight Reading, Scales, Chords, Hand Independence, and beloved melodies.

Structured graded syllabus (Trinity / ABRSM foundations)
Play Indian film songs, Western classics & devotional tunes
Individual instrument attention for kids & adult beginners
Strings & Rhythm

Guitar Class

Master Acoustic & Rhythm Guitar with hands-on training in Open & Barre Chords, Strumming Patterns, Fingerpicking, Tabs, and Lead Melodies.

Sing-along accompaniment & college band readiness
Malayalam, Hindi & English song chord progressions
Flexible evening & weekend slots for students & professionals
